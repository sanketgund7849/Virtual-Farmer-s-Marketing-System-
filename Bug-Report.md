## Severity Levels

- **High** – Major functionality is affected.
- **Medium** – Functionality is partially affected.
- **Low** – Minor UI or usability issue.

## Priority Levels

- **High** – Should be addressed quickly.
- **Medium** – Should be addressed after high-priority issues.
- **Low** – Can be addressed later.
